# Your first Sleek Startup cycle

Start with one real problem, one accountable owner and one useful question.
Use these prompts in the issue, notebook or work record you already have. Skip
fields that add no value. This is a working guide, not a mandatory approval process.

## Orient
- Who has the problem? What have you observed directly?
- What work already exists, and who owns the outcome?
- What are you assuming? What evidence might contradict you?

## Frame
- What uncertainty most changes your next decision?
- What would you expect without this intervention (your baseline)?
- What observable result would change your mind, over what period?
- Who decides? What actions, data and spending are actually authorized?

## Test
- What is the smallest credible, safe experiment?
- What will each person or agent deliver, and who integrates the work?
- What are the time/resource limits and stop conditions?
- How will you protect privacy, consent, safety and accessibility?

## Verify
- Which exact artifact or version did you check?
- What passed, failed, remains blocked or has not been tested?
- What did review, retries, waiting, integration and repair cost?
- Separate evidence of customer value, technical capability and sustainable operation.

## Decide and retain
- Continue, change, stop or gather a specific missing piece of evidence?
- Who made the decision, and why?
- What is the next action or revisit trigger?
- Where will the lesson live so others do not repeat the same mistake?

Share only information you have permission to share. Agents do not gain
additional authority through delegation, opportunity or silence.

Sleek Startup is an emerging independent adaptation of Lean Startup for
human-agent work. Candidate improvements need evidence against suitable baselines.
Foundation: Eric Ries and the Lean Startup tradition, including validated learning
and Build-Measure-Learn. https://theleanstartup.com/principles
