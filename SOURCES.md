## Source record

### S1. Eric Ries and Lean Startup

[The Lean Startup: Methodology](https://theleanstartup.com/principles), official overview, accessed 2026-11-03.

Source of the foundational Build–Measure–Learn loop, validated learning, MVP experiments, actionable measures, innovation accounting and pivot-or-persevere decisions. The framework uses original wording to adapt those ideas to human–agent work. Lean Startup already addresses disciplined learning and measurement; these are not innovations attributed to Sleek Startup. The agent-specific mechanisms are proposals for testing, not evidence that Lean Startup lacks rigor or that this adaptation is superior. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

### S2. Exo repository ownership and review workflow

[Repository workflow at commit dde4e0d](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exo/blob/dde4e0d853df950ab6c35a76c0612d2f8bd08e68/docs/REPOSITORY-WORKFLOW.md) and [repository routing instructions at the same commit](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/exo/blob/dde4e0d853df950ab6c35a76c0612d2f8bd08e68/AGENTS.md).

Documented practice: canonical shared implementation, preserving author ownership, explicit handoffs, independent exact-head review, separate downstream verification and clear distinctions between landing, integration and live installation. Some sections of the source are explicitly advisory. This framework borrows the general coordination mechanism; it does not change that project's authority or adopt every detail of its procedure.

### S3. Contextual product decisions

[Compass PR #12](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/compass/pull/12), head [e455870](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/compass/blob/e45587015624c191046d88da9aaba5e1793cc6d3/PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md).

Open, ready-for-review proposal when inspected. It records founder direction to make product choices when relevant, with real evidence, reversible defaults, triggers, ownership and early protection where delay would be harmful. Its existence supports the direction and proposed mechanism, not adoption or measured results. Product-specific applications remain with Compass and the relevant component owners.

### S4. Small slices and contracts

[Compass architecture and roadmap at commit 6a3cf9a](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/compass/blob/6a3cf9ae62a0c50107580eb831699503f0f94ff7/SILICONCORDAT.md).

A proposal for small end-to-end slices, traceable contributions and testing contracts across implementations before broadening them. ## What is original here

The organizing language, combined operating loop, field guide and cycle note are a proposed synthesis for this project. Principles such as reversible choices, small batches, explicit ownership and careful evaluation have broader histories; combining them does not establish novelty. Add primary attribution when a specific additional method is introduced. Do not copy protected text, diagrams or proprietary operating material without appropriate permission.

## Contribution standard

A useful contribution supplies a concrete mechanism and its context, not another slogan. Explain what it helps decide, how it works, when it fails, what it costs and what evidence supports it. Use the inventory's evidence states. State source versions and retain contrary evidence. Separate a recommendation from a result and a result from a general claim.

Keep changes focused and reviewable. Check existing work first, preserve ownership and link related changes. Validate relative links and whitespace; verify cited sources and status. For operational examples, check confidentiality and publication authority before including them. Do not infer permission from repository access.

This repository contains documentation only at initialization, with no configured executable test suite or CI. Documentation checks support consistency and navigation, not proof that the methodology works. Future claims of improvement need real evaluations as described in the field guide.
